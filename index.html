How we trade

Five steps, one lot

The same sequence for a truckload or a vessel parcel. You see the numbers before you pay for the material.

  1. 01

    Meet

    We agree the material, the spec, the volume and the delivery point.

  2. 02

    Sample

    Our teams sample the lot. An assay sets the grade.

  3. 03

    Price

    We quote against the assay, in writing, for that lot.

  4. 04

    Load

    An independent inspector checks weight and quality at loading.

  5. 05

    Settle

    You receive the certificate of quality with the shipping documents.

Value

Who gains from a good trade

A lot that is sampled properly, paid fairly and delivered on spec is good for everyone who touches it.

At the source

Workers

Sampling, loading and haulage are skilled jobs. We use trained local teams and expect safe sites and fair, on-time pay wherever our material is handled.

Around the mine

Communities

We buy from small and mid-sized producers as well as large ones. That keeps mineral income in the towns where the material is mined.

At the other end

End users

Chrome becomes stainless steel. PGMs clean vehicle exhaust. Copper and cobalt carry and store electricity. A consistent grade from us means fewer surprises in your furnace.
